How it works

One conversation, four safeguards, one second.

1 · Speak“Mándale 500 baht a mi madre.” Any language.
2 · UnderstandClaude extracts intent, amount, currency, recipient.
3 · QuoteLocal amount → USDC, FX shown, zero fees.
4 · ConfirmNothing moves without a spoken “yes”. Big amounts ask a co-signer.
5 · SettleUSDC transfer on Avalanche. Sub-second finality, tx on screen.

Why this wins

Institutional rails (Canton, Stellar) move trillions, but nobody talks to them. Consumer apps have buttons, not conversations. U-bot Pay is the missing layer — built on the primitives the partners care about.
