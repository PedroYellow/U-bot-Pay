How it works

One conversation. Four safeguards. Under two seconds.

1 · Speak“Mándale 100 baht a mi madre.” Any of 90+ languages.
2 · UnderstandClaude extracts intent, amount, currency and recipient.
3 · QuoteLocal amount → USDC. FX shown. Zero fees.
4 · ConfirmNothing moves without a spoken “yes”. Big amounts ask a co-signer.
5 · SettleUSDC on Avalanche. Sub-second finality, tx on screen.

Why it wins

Institutional rails like Canton and Stellar move trillions, but nobody talks to them. Consumer apps have buttons, not conversations. u.bot Pay is the missing layer.
