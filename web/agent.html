How the money moves

Client requests the resource. Service replies 402 with payTo, amount, asset, network.
Client signs an EIP-712 TransferWithAuthorization (USDC's EIP-3009). No gas, no transaction yet — just a signature.
Client retries with X-PAYMENT: <signed authorization>.
Service recovers the signer, checks payee / amount / expiry / nonce, submits the authorization on-chain, and serves the response with an X-PAYMENT-RESPONSE receipt.
